Official guide

How to use this portal

This citizen support guide explains how to submit reports, review progress, and participate responsibly across public service and community problem-solving activities.

For citizens

  • Choose “Report a problem”.
  • Describe the community issue and its location.
  • Add a photo or approximate map location only if safe.
  • Track review status from “My activity”.

For student teams

  • Open an approved challenge.
  • Read the complete description and updates.
  • Submit a practical solution with your institution.
  • Wait for admin verification before it appears publicly.

For industry and mentors

  • Open a challenge matching your expertise.
  • Choose “Offer support”.
  • Describe mentorship, equipment, pilot access or another resource.
  • No payment is processed on this portal.

Safety and moderation

  • Never post phone numbers, passwords or private addresses.
  • All public problems, solutions and offers are reviewed.
  • Approximate coordinates are rounded before storage.
  • Uploaded photos should show the public problem area only.
Why is my submission pending? It is waiting for admin review to verify accuracy, safety, and usefulness.
Do I need an account? No. The prototype works without a formal sign-up, and activity is tracked in the browser.
Can an industry offer transfer money? No. This portal is for coordination, mentorship, and support, not payment processing.
Why does voice input not appear on my device? Voice support depends on browser permissions and device compatibility. Try a supported browser and allow microphone access.
