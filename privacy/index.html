Official notice

Your safety comes before visibility.

This platform follows a citizen-first privacy standard: we collect only what is needed for review, moderation, and public participation, and we protect personal information before any public visibility.

Information we store

When you submit something, this portal stores the text you provide, its review status, an anonymous browser key and the submission time. A problem report may also contain an optional photo and rounded approximate coordinates.

What becomes public

Only administrator-approved challenges, student solutions, industry offers and progress updates become visible. The anonymous browser key is never shown publicly.

What you should never submit

  • Passwords
  • Phone numbers
  • Personal email addresses
  • Government ID numbers
  • Private home addresses
  • Confidential institutional information

Location and photos

Location sharing is optional. Coordinates are rounded to reduce precision. Photos are limited by file type and size and are shown only after the related challenge is approved.

Moderation

Administrators may approve or reject submissions to reduce spam, unsafe content, false claims and personal-data exposure. An approval means the item is suitable for collaboration; it is not a government certification of every claim.

Browser-based tracking

“My activity” works using an anonymous key saved in your browser. If browser storage is cleared or another device is used, the old tracking history cannot be recovered through this prototype.

Prototype status

This is an educational civic platform. It does not process payments, create legal partnerships, provide emergency services or replace official government complaint channels.
